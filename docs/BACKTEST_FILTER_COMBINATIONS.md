## Method

- Indicators use the production formulas from `workers/yfinance/src/eyesinvest_worker/providers/analytics.py`; filter comparisons mirror `apps/web/src/lib/stocks/queries.ts :: applyScreenerFilters` (nulls never match, the `>` / `<` boundaries follow the live dropdowns).
- Signal at close T → entry at the close of T+1 → exit 21 sessions later. Equal weight, rebalanced every 21 sessions, no overlap between periods.
- The squeeze score is rebuilt point-in-time for every date (from the short-interest settlement known on that date) instead of the production worker's habit of writing today's score onto every historical row. The HK morning-session component is dropped because AM short volume only exists from 2026-08.
- `Alpha/mo` = average 21-session forward return of the picks minus the same figure for the whole market universe.
- `Seg 1/2/3` = Sharpe over three equal sub-periods — a stable edge is positive in all three.
- **Credibility gate**: ≥5 names in the basket on a typical rebalance, ≥12 monthly periods with a position, ≥500 stock-level observations.
- **Multiple testing** — the single biggest caveat. 18,303 combinations were scored per market, so the best full-sample Sharpe is mostly selection noise: the median combination already prints a positive net Sharpe. Only the *selective strategies* table is trustworthy, and only because it ranks on the train half and then reads two unseen halves (validation + test). Treat a combination that survives that as a candidate, not as evidence.
- **Nine filters** are covered — 1M return, Crowded, Squeeze, MA5, MA20, 1M Green Share, 1M Push/Pull Ease, Volume Efficiency and 30D Drawdown — at every threshold the live dropdowns offer, combined up to 4 at a time. Every combination maps to a paste-ready screener URL.
- `shares_outstanding` is a current snapshot (it powers the volume-efficiency filter); survivorship bias applies (currently active stocks only), the window is 2023-08 → 2026-10, there is no liquidity or market-cap screen, and max drawdown is measured on monthly closes only.
